Get your RefQuest account game‑ready

A step-by-step guide to RQ+ for CWLOA members attending the McKendree clinic. Work through it on your phone before clinic day.

  • For: CWLOA members new to RQ+
  • Time: about 15 minutes
  • Revised: October 5, 2026

Before you arrive

Seven things to have done. Each one links to its step below.

  1. Sign in to RQ+ and open the CWLOA portal.
  2. Check that your phone number and email are current.
  3. Block every date you cannot work, and share blocks with each assigning group.
  4. Set a daily mileage limit, or leave it blank for no limit.
  5. Find where your assignments show up.
  6. Open RQ Pay and finish setup yourself, inside RQ+.
  7. Save the support contacts in your phone.

1

Sign in and open the CWLOA portal

Everything else in this guide starts here.

  1. Go to plus.refquest.com and sign in with the email address CWLOA has on file for you.
  2. Forgot your password? Use the reset link on the sign-in screen. Never send your password to a clinician, assigner, or anyone else.
  3. If you work for more than one group, RQ+ shows more than one portal. Pick CWLOA for anything about this clinic.
Do this before clinic day. A password reset on the field eats time you need for warm-ups.
2

Check your contact details

Assigners use these to reach you about game changes.

  1. Click your avatar in the upper right corner.
  2. Open Profile & Settings.
  3. Confirm your phone number and email. Fix anything old and save.
3

Block the dates you can't work

An open date tells an assigner you are available. Blocks prevent turnbacks.

  1. Click ASSIGN, then CALENDAR.
  2. Select the + icon in the bottom left of the day you want to block.
  3. Choose FIRM or PROVISIONAL as the block type.
  4. Add block notes and preferences if you need them.
  5. Toggle ON (orange) every account that should see this block.
  6. Click CREATE.

You cannot place a firm block on a day where you already have an active assignment in that account.

A block only counts for the groups you share it with. One assigner put it this way: the date may have been blocked, but it was not blocked to me, so it shows as though you are open.
4

Copy your blocks to every assigning group

If you work several conferences, this saves you from entering each block twice.

  1. On the Calendar page, find the icon near the top right that looks like a raised rectangle with dots on its left and bottom edges.
  2. Click it to open block copying.
  3. Select the assigning group you are copying from.
  4. Toggle ON (orange) the group you are copying to.
  5. Click UPDATE BLOCKS.
  6. Repeat for each group, then open the destination calendar and confirm the blocks are there.
5

Set your daily mileage limits

This tells assigners how far you are willing to drive on a given day.

  1. Click your avatar in the upper right corner.
  2. Open Profile & Settings, then Mileage Limits.
  3. Set a limit for each day of the week, for each assigning group, in each portal.
  4. Click Save Changes before you switch portals or leave the page.

The default is no limit. Leave a day blank if you will travel any distance that day.

Mileage limit is not travel pay. The limit only controls which games you are offered. Travel money, if any, is set by the group that pays the game. RQ+ does not set it, and this guide does not quote rates.
6

Find and answer your assignments

Assigners build crews around your answers, so answer early.

  1. New offers and accepted games appear in RQ+, and RQ+ also sends email notices.
  2. Open each offer. Check the date, start time, time zone, site, and your crew.
  3. Accept or decline in RQ+ by the deadline your assigner sets.
  4. Watch for game-change notices. Re-open the game whenever one arrives.
  5. Can't work a game you accepted? Contact your assigner right away. Follow that group's turnback rules.
Response deadlines and turnback penalties come from each assigning group. Check their RQ+ Resources, not this page.
7

Set up RQ Pay

RQ Pay sends game fees to your bank by direct deposit.

  1. Open RQ Pay in RQ+ and follow the enrollment steps on screen.
  2. Enter your bank details yourself, inside RQ+ only. RefQuest confirms new accounts with two small test deposits.
  3. Look for your payment history inside RQ Pay to see what has been paid. RQ Pay also produces your 1099.
Never send bank, tax, or Social Security details by email or text, and never to a clinician. Pay timing and amounts come from the group that pays the game.
